Who may touch the real manuscript (main)? Three settings

Same collaboration — the only difference is the setting on the door

Setting 1
Wide open

A PR is good manners;
you press merge yourself

Peer co-authors who trust
each other — our class is here

Even a direct push to main is fine.

→

Setting 2
PR required

Raise your hand, show the diff
— no waiting for sign-off

A team that wants every
change on the record

You cannot edit main without a PR.

→

Setting 3
Approval required

Only the named person’s nod
lights up the merge button

Advisor × graduate student
co-writing a thesis

Students cannot approve their own PR —
the key sits with the advisor.

A PR = raising your hand + putting the line-by-line diff on the table. The lock is a setting on the door, changeable any time.
